The concept

One mountain. One river. One lake.

A point-to-point adventure beginning at New Dungeon Ghyll, climbing Lingmoor Fell, descending to Skelwith Bridge and aiming to reach the northern end of Windermere by packraft before exploring Waterhead and Ambleside.

⚠️ Critical equipment limit
Your Decathlon packraft is not certified above Grade 2. A published Grade 2–3 rapid before Brathay Pool prevents treating Skelwith Bridge → Windermere as a continuous approved descent. A safe upstream exit, portage and re-entry have NOT been verified. Do not launch on the assumption that you can bypass it.

Before entering the water

Go / no-go gates

✓ Only launch if

River level and current conditions are suitable; all hazards have been assessed; a lawful safe exit before the Grade 2–3 rapid is confirmed; the group has the necessary skills, equipment and rescue arrangements.

Mandatory checks

✕ Abort if

The rapid cannot be safely avoided, the bank is inaccessible, trees obstruct the channel, water is high, daylight is insufficient, or Windermere is too windy or exposed for your boat.
