The route

The horseshoe links the high fells surrounding the Langdale valley. The exact line, distance and ascent should be taken from the expedition GPX rather than assumed from a generic circuit.

Mountain conditions can change quickly. Check the latest forecast, daylight, visibility and any ice before setting out. Carry suitable navigation and emergency equipment.

Day 02 / Amphibious adventure

Lingmoor Fell &
Brathay Packrafting

An early breakfast, a drive to Ambleside, a bus to Skelwith Bridge and a downstream packraft journey, finishing with time to explore Windermere.

🍳 Breakfast 07:30🚌 Planned bus 09:25🛶 Packraft📍 Skelwith → Windermere

Expedition sequence

  1. 07:30 — Breakfast. Eat early and prepare equipment for the river day.
  2. Drive to Ambleside. Park, organise the packrafts and reach the bus stop in good time.
  3. 09:25 — Planned service 516 bus. From Ambleside Kelsick Road to Skelwith Bridge (09:35 on the published summer 2026 timetable; reconfirm for the actual date).
  4. 09:35–10:15 — Launch preparation. Find a suitable lawful access point downstream of Skelwith Force; inspect hazards, river level and conditions; inflate the boats.
  5. Approximately 10:15–12:30 — River Brathay. Paddle towards Windermere only where the water is within the crew's skills and the craft's Grade 2 limit; scout and portage as needed.
  6. Approximately 12:30–14:00 — Windermere. Potter around the lake margin if wind and conditions permit, stop for lunch and enjoy the scenery.
  7. Afternoon — Return. Land at a suitable agreed take-out, pack away the boats, return to the car in Ambleside and head home.
Important safety note: Skelwith Force is a serious waterfall hazard: launch downstream, never above it. Other Brathay sections may exceed Grade 2. Do not assume a continuous navigable Grade 2 route. Scout, portage or abandon sections as needed. Check river levels, access rights, wind and boat traffic. This is an expedition concept, not a verified river pilot.
